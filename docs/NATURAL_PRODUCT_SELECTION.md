# Natural-product selection

The built-in `Natural Product Exploration` preset treats the Dockability
Envelope as an operational eligibility layer. Lipinski, Veber, Ghose, Egan,
Muegge and Beyond Rule of Five are classifications or informative outputs
unless the user deliberately promotes one to hard exclusion. PAINS and Brenk
are warnings by default.

The Dockability Envelope defaults are editable operational limits for a
specified docking workflow, not universal biological thresholds.
